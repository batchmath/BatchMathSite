CHANGES
-------
1. Derivatives Comprehensive Review — Conceptual & AP-Style
- Removed the low-value implicit linear family that could display prompts such as 1x + 1y = c.
- Conceptual pool now emphasizes differentiability/continuity, derivative interpretation,
  first/second derivative signs, inverse-derivative reasoning, nondifferentiability,
  local-extremum sign changes, motion interpretation, and tangent-line approximation meaning.

2. Equations of Tangent and Normal Lines
- Given functions suppress coefficients of 1 (x rather than 1x, -x rather than -1x).
- Point-slope answer formatting also suppresses unnecessary slope coefficients of 1.
- Fixed answer-choice focus behavior through the shared AP topic-practice renderer: after an
  answer is checked, focus moves to Next Question so Enter advances consistently whether the
  user clicked directly on MathJax/text inside a choice or empty space in the choice button.
- Existing tangent/normal variety retained and cleaned.

3. Horizontal and Vertical Tangent Lines
- Expanded to 9 observed variants spanning shifted cubic/quartic, trig, rational,
  logarithmic, exponential, cube-root, fifth-root, and root-plus-linear families.
- Answer choices are mathematical/MathJax-rendered and worked explanations use MathJax-safe notation.

4. Horizontal and Vertical Tangent Lines Implicitly
- Rebuilt from the old ellipse-heavy pool to 13 observed variants.
- Families now include ellipses, hyperbolas, mixed quadratics, cubic implicit curves,
  trigonometric implicit curves, exponential implicit curves, and product-style implicit curves.
- Horizontal and vertical tangent questions both represented.
- Fractions in answer choices/explanations are MathJax-safe; no raw frac artifacts.
- Matching Unit 3 comprehensive-review generator use is automatically synchronized.
