Reciprocal / Quotient

\(\sin x=\frac1{\csc x},\quad \cos x=\frac1{\sec x},\quad \tan x=\frac1{\cot x}\)

\(\tan x=\frac{\sin x}{\cos x},\quad \cot x=\frac{\cos x}{\sin x}\)

Pythagorean

\(\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1\)

\(1+\tan^2x=\sec^2x,\quad 1+\cot^2x=\csc^2x\)

Double Angle / Power Reduction

\(\sin 2x=2\sin x\cos x\)

\(\cos 2x=\cos^2x-\sin^2x=1-2\sin^2x=2\cos^2x-1\)

\(\sin^2x=\frac12(1-\cos 2x),\quad \cos^2x=\frac12(1+\cos 2x)\)

Even / Odd

\(\sin(-x)=-\sin x,\quad \cos(-x)=\cos x,\quad \tan(-x)=-\tan x\)

\(\csc(-x)=-\csc x,\quad \sec(-x)=\sec x,\quad \cot(-x)=-\cot x\)
