# BatchMath v10.7.12 Representative Problems

## Introduction to Limits

- A single graph on the closed domain `[-6,6]` asks, in sequence, for three to five left-hand, right-hand, and two-sided limits without asking for any point value.
- A table omits `x=a` and asks for an estimated one-sided or two-sided limit from nearby values.

## Limits of Continuous Functions

- `lim_(x→π/6) sin(2x+π/6)`
- `lim_(x→1) root(5, x²+2)`
- `lim_(x→0) ln(2+e^x)`
- `lim_(x→0) 3^(x²−1)`

## Understanding sin(1/x)

- `lim_(x→0) e^x sin(k/x)` with distinct approach sequences establishing DNE.
- `lim_(x→a) (x−a)^m cos(k/(x−a))` with a vanishing absolute-value bound.
- Bounds tending to different values, followed by the accurate conclusion that squeeze is inconclusive and a separate oscillation argument is required.

## One-Sided Limits

- Absolute-value products in which cancellation depends on the approach side.
- Nonconstant rational numerators with repeated odd/even denominator factors.
- Removable factors and cancellations that still leave a pole.
- Linked left, right, and two-sided questions using one unchanged piecewise or rational function.
- Root/logarithm endpoints for which only the domain-admissible side exists.
