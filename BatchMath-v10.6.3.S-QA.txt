BASELINE
Complete cumulative site built from v10.6.3.R. R's generation counting and

CHANGES
Advanced Trigonometric Limits now provides a Show Hint / Hide Hint button.
Each of the 38 problem families has a targeted identity or algebra hint.
Hints preserve the question and typed answer, do not reveal the final answer,
and do not affect attempted/correct counts. New Question and Next reset hints.

All 38 families now have numbered explanations with display equations.
Explanations show the identity/factorization, the regrouping, standard-limit
justification, and coefficient arithmetic. The 1-cosine limit is derived using
conjugate/Pythagorean algebra instead of being stated as a second-order fact.
Wrong answers reveal the method; correct answers keep Show Method available.
A submitted Enter remains on feedback; the next Enter advances once.
Invalid-input feedback does not accidentally enable Enter-to-New.

The standalone HTML and conversation tester use a byte-identical copy of the
production generator, hint, answer-checking, and explanation controller.
They also use the production fraction normalizer, seeded RNG, and shared
keyboard block. Their surrounding shell omits site navigation, analytics
transport, and service-worker registration. MathJax requires internet access.
A repeatable standalone bundler is included in tools/.
