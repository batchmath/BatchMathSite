# BatchMath v10.7.14 Representative Problems

## Nine Key Features

- A piecewise-linear graph with an open left endpoint asks for an increasing interval whose left delimiter must be a parenthesis.
- An x-intercept question includes the graph's actual y-intercept as a distractor, and a y-intercept question includes the actual x-intercept.

## Classifying Discontinuities

- A multi-branch piecewise function combines removable and infinite behavior with safely rendered `\\lt`, `\\gt`, and `\\ge` conditions.
- Powered sine and cosine cancellation families suppress exponent 1 while retaining their standard-limit order explanations.

## Intermediate Value Theorem

- An unfactored cubic of the form `(x-r)(x²+1)` is presented in expanded form and evaluated at endpoints on opposite sides of `r`.
- A trig quotient `sin(kx)/x` is checked on a positive interval with familiar-angle endpoint values.
- Rational examples distinguish a pole outside the interval, an interior pole, and an interior removable hole.
- Shifted radical and logarithmic examples contrast a valid closed interval with one containing an excluded endpoint.
- A rational-times-root problem asks which proposed interval satisfies both the real-domain and continuity hypotheses.
