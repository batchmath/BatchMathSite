# BatchMath v10.7.8 Representative Problems

1. `lim(x→0) e^x sin(1/x)` is DNE. The amplitude tends to 1; approaches making sine tend to 1 and −1 make the products tend to different values.
2. `lim(x→0) x² cos(1/x³)=0`. Since the absolute value is at most `x²`, both squeeze bounds tend to 0.
3. From `−e^x ≤ e^x sin(1/x) ≤ e^x`, squeeze is inconclusive because the bounds do not share a limit. DNE follows separately from persistent oscillation.
4. For `c(x−a)|x−a|/(x−a)²`, cancel one factor. From the left, `|x−a|=−(x−a)`, so the limit is `−c`; from the right it is `c`; the two-sided limit is DNE.
5. For `−(x−a)/(x−a)²` from the right, cancellation leaves `−1/(x−a)`, so the limit is `−∞`.
6. If piecewise branches both approach 4 but `f(a)=−2`, both one-sided limits and the two-sided limit are 4, while the function is not continuous at `a`.
7. `lim(x→3−) ln(3−x)=−∞`. The two-sided limit is DNE because no real-domain points approach from the right under the course convention.
