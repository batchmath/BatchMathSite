# v13.0.A experimental 3D cross sections: test results

The feature is described in `docs/v13.0.A-cross-sections-3d.md`. All results are from the final code on
`claude/v13.0a-cross-sections-3d`, branched from `main` at `9dbb4f8` (v12.9.G).

## Geometry and mathematics (Node)

**`tools/review-lab/unit6/qa/solid3d.mjs`** generated 1,440 Volume by Cross Sections problems: every family, tier and
calculator kind, with and without the "Perpendicular to the x-axis" Practice Type.
- **Descriptor.** It is attached to the 960 non-calculator problems and to no calculator problem. It is never
  enumerable and never reaches stored data.
- **Support.** The model is offered for 146 problems: square cross sections perpendicular to the x-axis, in all 11
  regions (cap 16, half cap 9, semicircle 9, disk 8, triangle 12, pair 9, under a root 7, under a power 15, left of a
  power 6, eˣ with y = 1 31, the two-piece region 24). All 146 validate. The other 814 are refused.
- **Volume.** The closed mesh's volume, by the divergence theorem, matches each problem's own answer (its exact value
  or its setup's value) within 0.063%. An independent integral of s(x)² matches to 10⁻¹³.
- **A(x).** Typed A(x) answers match s(x)² of the model at 238 sample points.
- **Squares.** All 1,460 drawn squares (the outlines and the highlighted one) have side top(x) − bot(x) in both
  directions, lie in the plane of their x, and span from the lower curve to the upper one.
- **Placement.** The highlighted square is at the 2D picture's slice.
- **Samples.** Every sample of the base and walls lies on the boundary functions.

**No other change.**
- Seed by seed, 2,070 problems from every Unit 6 engine are identical to `main` in question, prompts, answers,
  grading data and explanations.

## Browser: the 3D model

**`tools/review-lab/unit6/cs3d-browser.mjs`** uses Chromium and the real page, at 1366×900 with a mouse and 390×844
with touch. For each of the 11 regions it forced a square-on-x problem through the page's own generator (22 runs).
Every check below passed in every run.
- **Button.** "Show 3D Model (Beta)" appears once, closed, directly after the 2D "Show Graph" control.
- **2D graph.** Show Graph still opens its graph.
- **Lazy loading.** three.js is not requested before the model is opened.
- **Opening.** The button reads "Hide 3D Model" and one canvas is drawn, with non-background pixels and yellow pixels
  from the highlighted square.
- **The drawn highlighted square**, read back from the GPU buffers, stands at the 2D slice's x, spans y from bot(x)
  to top(x) and z from 0 to s(x), with equal side and height. Examples:
  - y = 9 − x²: 8.7696 at x = −0.48;
  - the disk: 1.9742;
  - the two-piece region: 3.6661 at x = 3.36;
  - y = xᵖ beside y = H: 24.9996.
- **The drawn solid** spans [a, b] and the curves' lowest and highest y, and reaches the largest s.
- **Labels.** The labels x, y, z and s are present.
- **Controls.**
  - Mouse drag rotates and the wheel zooms in.
  - One-finger touch rotates and a two-finger pinch zooms in, through real touch events.
  - Reset view restores the starting view.
  - There are 0 redraws while idle.
- **Hiding** releases the model. Quick repeated toggling never leaves more than one canvas.
- **Answers.** A typed answer is graded correct with the model open, and the model stays open.
- **Layout.** No page is wider than the screen, and the model stays inside the question box.
- **Stale geometry.** With a model open, the next question removes it. The new supported question gets a closed
  toggle for that problem.
- **Unsupported problems** show no toggle and no canvas, even right after a model was open: a semicircle, a quarter
  circle, an equilateral triangle, and squares perpendicular to the y-axis. Their 2D graph still works.
- **Calculator page.** It loads no 3D module and shows no toggle.
- **Errors.** There were 0 console errors.

Screenshots, reviewed visually: the cap, the disk and the two-piece region at both sizes. Each solid has the expected
shape, for example the ridge of the two-piece region peaking where its lower boundary changes.

  errors.
