# v12.8.X revisions (from v12.8.W)

A targeted follow-up to the v12.8.W Unit 5 rebuild: strict rounding for numeric answers, separate calculator and
non-calculator practice, Rate Problems refocused on accumulation, clearer Motion distance and Separable
explanations, and Calculator and Non-Calculator versions of the Comprehensive Review. Everything else from W
(Units 1–4, IM1, the rest of Unit 5) is unchanged unless listed here. Development log: `docs/v12.8.X-progress.md`.

## Strict rounding (all Unit 5 calculator answers)
- An answer that says "round to three decimal places", "to the nearest cent" or "to the nearest whole number" must
  equal the correctly rounded value, rounded half away from zero (1.2345 → 1.235, −1.2345 → −1.235). W accepted
  anything within 0.001 of the unrounded value, so 1.234 passed for 1.2346; it is now wrong.
- Accepted forms: `.923` or `0.923`, `-0`, trailing zeros (`1.25`, `1.250`, `1.2500`), `$1.2` for $1.20, thousands
  commas and a unit word. Graded wrong: a neighbouring value and an unrounded value with extra digits (`1.2503` when
  the answer rounds to 1.250). An expression such as `ln(2)/5` is not graded; the student is asked for the rounded
  decimal and can re-enter it.
- The same rule applies to calculator interval endpoints and rounded lists. Exact answers are unchanged.

## Calculator and non-calculator practice
- New pages: Basic First-Order Differential Equations and Motion Problems each have Calculator Practice
  (`calculator-practice/`) and Non-Calculator Practice (`non-calculator-practice/`) next to the existing page, which
  keeps its URL as Mixed Practice. Topic pages show the three buttons.
- The filter works at the generator level: a calculator page only draws calculator-active families and tiers, a
  non-calculator page only the others; the result is verified, and a request no family can satisfy raises an error
  rather than quietly showing other problems. Practice Type menus list only eligible types.

## Rate Problems: accumulation only
- Families and Mixed weights: accumulate one rate 25%, initial amount plus or minus change 25%, inflow and outflow
  25%, delayed starts and piecewise rates 15%, connected accumulation problems 10%.
- Removed from every mode and from the reviews: the Increasing, Decreasing and Extreme Amounts type; the derivative
  of a rate (for example E'(2) in the parking-garage problem); the net rate at an instant; greatest and least
  amounts; when a total reaches a target; how much accumulates while a rate is above a level. These belong to a
  future FRQ-style section.
- New: comparison of two windows and clock-time windows (calculator); multi-part problems made only of accumulation
  parts (inflow total, outflow total, net change, amounts).
- New Rate Calculator Practice and Non-Calculator Practice pages. Non-calculator problems integrate polynomial,
  square-root, A + B·cos(πt/k) / sin(πt/k) and c/(t+1) rates exactly in every family and tier.
- Calculator pages for Basic and Motion have more variety: Basic — values from an initial condition, the starting
  value, net change and two values (homework-style points such as π/6); Motion — position and average velocity,
  displacement and total distance (every sign change found), velocity / acceleration / speed, rest and direction,
  on all three tiers.

## Clearer explanations
- Motion total distance: after finding where v changes sign and testing each piece, the explanation adds the
  integrals of the positive pieces as they are and takes absolute values only of the negative pieces
  (Total distance = ∫₀² v dt + |∫₂⁴ v dt| + ∫₄⁶ v dt). Answers are unchanged.
- Separable: when the constant is a logarithm, the explanation shows the exponent laws
  (e^{x + ln 3} = e^x · e^{ln 3} = 3e^x, e^{3x² − ln 4} = e^{3x²}/4) and resolves |y| = 3e^x as y = 3e^x or
  y = −3e^x using the initial condition, with the same care for |y − a|, |x − h|, |ln x| and |sin y|.
