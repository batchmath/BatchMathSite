# v12.8.V revisions (from v12.8.U)

Polish for the final four Unit 4 integrals engines, a repair of the FTC answer checker, and the FTC
landing-page practice buttons. All v12.8.U work is kept; v12.8.V is built on top of it.

1. Scrollbars on short mathematics. Every equation line in the Basic, U-Substitution, Natural Log /
   Inverse Trig and MVT explanations (and some Unit 1 explanations) showed a small vertical scrollbar.
   The boxes used `overflow-x:auto`, which makes `overflow-y` auto as well, and MathJax glyph boxes
   extend a few pixels past the line box, so each line became a scroll box. The four engines and their
   Comprehensive Review topics now use the natural-height Unit 4 layout that Geometry, FTC and Integral
   Rules already use: equations grow to fit, and long ones wrap at `=`, `+` and `-` (each integral is
   kept whole) instead of scrolling; short math stays on one line. The shared question box and the
   Unit 1 explanation keep sideways scrolling for wide mathematics but no longer get a vertical scroll
   axis, and the explanation has room below its last line so nothing is cut off.
2. Inverse-trig arguments are reduced. `\tan^{-1}(4x/2)` is now `\tan^{-1}(2x)`, `(3x/6)` is `x/2`, and
   the same for `\sin^{-1}`, in the answer, the explanation and the Comprehensive Review. Unreduced
   equivalent answers are still accepted. Also removed: multiplication by 1, powers of 1 (`u^1`) and the
   `1 \cdot` line when the MVT interval has length 1. The audit now lints the displayed TeX for these.
3. FTC checker. A product typed with the keypad, such as 2, x, then sec (serialized `2xsec(x^(6))`),
   was rejected as "Enter a valid exact expression in x", because the shared expression parser read
   `xsec` as one unknown name. The parser now takes a leading x, y, e or pi from such a run and reads
   the function after it (unknown letters are still invalid), and the FTC checker marks a factor written
   directly before a function as a product. About 31% of generated FTC answers have this shape. Wrong
   derivatives are still rejected. The FTC numeric comparison no longer accepts 2× the answer when the
   answer is tiny at every probe. New permanent test: `npm run qa:ftc-checker` (also in `qa:nobrowser`).
4. Fundamental Theorem of Calculus and Integral Rules page: the two practice buttons have their own
   section after the resources (Fundamental Theorem of Calculus Practice, then Combining Integral Rules
   Practice). Destinations, assignments, answer keys and videos are unchanged.
5. Inverse-trig keys (sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹, tan⁻¹) re-verified: they insert asin( / acos( / atan(, can be
   edited and navigated, are accepted by the checkers, and fit at phone width. Keypads that cannot need
   them (product and quotient rule) do not show them.
