# BatchMath v12.8.R (Claude build)

Incremental revision of v12.8.Q (see v12.8.Q-revisions.md).

- Chain Rule page: the two videos were removed from "Additional Examples" and are now Homework Walkthrough Video buttons on the last two homework assignments ("Finding Derivatives at a Point..." and "Nested Chain Rule Derivatives"). Mapping is by the order the videos were supplied (see the delivery notes); swap the two ids if the assignments are reversed.
- Every derivative homework walkthrough button now reads exactly "HW Walkthrough Video" (Derivatives with Tables and Quotient Rule were changed back).
- FTC engine: answers are built by a small exact simplifier (`CAS` in assets/ap-topic-generators.js). It combines powers and coefficients, applies powers to monomials, reduces cos(-u) and sin(-u), merges exponentials, and multiplies out only polynomial factors. The displayed answer, every line of the worked solution, and the expected value used by the checker all come from the same expression tree.
- Trig notation: sin^2 x, tan^2 x, sec^2(2x), and so on, in questions, feedback, steps and answers.
- Answer checking (assets/unit4-exact-answers.js): accepts sin² x, sin^2(3x), sin^(2)(x), tan x, superscript digits, and spaces as multiplication. Exact symbolic comparison first; if that cannot decide, agreement at 12+ domain points with a purely relative tolerance.
- New QA: tools/q-ftc-simplify-qa.mjs, tools/q-ftc-keypad-browser-qa.mjs.
