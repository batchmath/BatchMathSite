# v12.8.Y revisions (from final v12.8.X)

A strictly scoped Unit 5 cleanup: only items 1–5 of the v12.8.Y prompt, plus the version, tests, packaging and
these notes. Everything else is as in final v12.8.X (`ffe7d7c`). Development log: `docs/v12.8.Y-progress.md`.

## What changed, item by item
1. **Combined Mixed pages retired.** Basic First-Order, Motion and Rate keep only Calculator Practice and
   Non-Calculator Practice; the Comprehensive Review keeps only the Calculator Review and the Non-Calculator
   Review, as two equal buttons. The old URLs (`…/basic-first-order-differential-equations/practice/`,
   `…/motion-problems/practice/`, `…/rate-problems/practice/`, `…/comprehensive-review/assignment-practice/`)
   are now small pages with no practice engine that send the student straight to the topic page (where they
   choose Calculator or Non-Calculator). They are `noindex`, point their canonical link at the topic page and are
   no longer in the sitemap or on any button. The review pages' context line no longer says "Differential
   Equations Mixed". (`tools/build-unit5-pages.py`, the four pages, the four topic pages, `sitemap.xml`.)
2. **Basic Calculator fraction coefficients.** In the Basic calculator derivative formatter only, a scalar times
   one stacked fraction goes into the numerator: `\frac{3\sin x}{x}`, `-\frac{2e^{x}}{x}`, `\frac{4}{\ln x}`;
   coefficient −1 gives `-\frac{…}{…}` and 1 gives the fraction alone. The function, the answer and every other
   word of the question and explanation are unchanged. (`assets/unit5-basic.js`, `fracParts` and
   `calcDerivative`.)
3. **Basic Calculator: one question family, no dropdown.** Only "find the value at another point from a known
   value" (`calc_value`) remains, with its derivative forms, points (forward and backward), values and tiers. The
   retired `calc_initial` and `calc_two_point` are no longer registered, so neither Calculator Practice nor the
   Calculator Review can produce them. The page has no Practice Type dropdown. Non-calculator Basic content is
   unchanged. (`assets/unit5-basic.js`, the page.)
4. **Calculator total distance as the integral of speed.** The explanation now reads "Total distance is the
   integral of speed. Speed is the absolute value of velocity, |v(t)|, so the calculator evaluates the definite
   integral of speed numerically", then Total distance = ∫₀ᵀ speed dt = ∫₀ᵀ |v(t)| dt = ∫₀ᵀ |…| dt ≈ answer. No
   zeros, sign tests or pieces are shown; the value is still computed by splitting at the zeros, so answers,
   rounding and grading are unchanged. The wrong-answer note for |displacement| on that part now says to
   integrate |v(t)| instead of splitting. Displacement and every non-calculator explanation are unchanged.
   (`assets/unit5-motion.js`, `famCalcDistance`.)
5. **Compact Practice Type menus.** Each engine lists its Practice Types (`MODES`): the existing families a choice
   draws from (optionally only some tiers) and, for one choice, a test on the generated problem. Only listed
   choices are accepted; any other value (a retired selector, or a choice of the other calculator kind) gives the
   page's whole pool, never another kind. "All Problems" is the page's whole pool with X's families and weights
   (identical problems to X for the same seed). (`assets/unit5-core.js` `makeEngine`, the six engines,
   `assets/unit5-review.js`, the builder and pages.)

## Teacher acceptance checklist (v12.8.Y only)
1. Combined calculator/non-calculator Mixed Practice pages and Mixed Review gone; separate calc/noncalc review
   buttons and engines remain, legacy bookmarks take the student to a choice page.
2. Basic Calculator fraction coefficient is INSIDE numerator on all affected fractional derivative templates,
   including signs and coefficients of 1.
3. Basic Calculator produces ONLY find-a-value-given-a-known-value problems, has no Practice Type dropdown, and the
   other two calculator question families are no longer generated (including in Calculator Review).
4. Calculator total-distance explanations use integral of SPEED ∫|v(t)|dt, without showing sign-interval
   decomposition; answers correct and unchanged.
5. Every Unit 5 dropdown matches the exact compact choices above, and the Separable and Basic Calculator dropdowns
   are absent, while all authorized problem types and difficulty variety survive.
6. Separate Calculator Review and Non-Calculator Review remain easy to find, with only All Review Problems /
   Foundational / Intermediate / Challenging as selections, and with no cross-mode problems.
