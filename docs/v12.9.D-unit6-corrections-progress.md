## Correction 2: semicircles on their diameter

`qa:unit6` section 5c checks the following:
- **The old variant is gone.** No `semicircle_r` remains in the shapes or the shape pool. In 1,440 generated problems
  (cross sections, multipart and both reviews), no question, prompt, explanation or answer uses a radius-across
  semicircle.
- **Problem counts.** The sample has 98 semicircle problems and 52 quarter-circle problems.
- **Independent volumes.** On 296 regions and directions, π/8 and π/4 match an independent integral of s². The
  reference uses Simpson's rule after a smoothing substitution; it is not the engine's own computation. Exact and
  numeric values agree.
- **Setup coefficients.** Semicircle setups use π/8 and quarter-circle setups use π/4.
- **Pictures.** All 8 cross-section pictures match their definitions. The semicircle's arc spans the highlighted slice
  as its diameter, with a radius of s/2 from the centre and the labels d = s and s/2. The quarter circle's radius is
  the slice. Squares, rectangles and triangles are drawn in proportion.
- **The teacher's case** (y = −x² + 4, y = 0, perpendicular to the y-axis) is V = 4π:
  - 4π is accepted, and 16π is refused;
  - the setup (π/8)∫₀⁴(2√(4 − y))² dy is accepted, and the π/2 setup is refused.
- **On v12.9.C** this section fails: the variant still exists, and radius-across problems appear.

**Seed-by-seed comparison.** The only answer changes are problems that used to draw the radius-across semicircle,
each now with the diameter: 16 cross-section and 17 multipart problems in the compared samples. The shell display fix
accounts for the rest.
