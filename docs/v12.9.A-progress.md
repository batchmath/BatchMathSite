## Fixes found by these checks (before the QA commit)
- The bounds step of every explanation was empty: regions dropped their bounds text and extents.
- The cubic crossing-the-axis problems (the tests' 4x³ − 16x² + 12x) were never produced: a map index became a zero
  denominator.
- Signed pieces read "+ −∫", and one bounds text read "+ −6".
- A typed integrand with a pole could make the checker run for many seconds. It is now limited to 400,000
  evaluations; the engines' own integrals need at most about 17,000.
- In one wedge region a cross-section setup without the square had the right value. Problems where a common wrong
  setup ties are no longer generated.
- The shell setup under y = 2/x has a constant integrand and was rejected. It is now accepted on the model's interval
  only.
- The 2025–26 Test A graph has no local maximum, which the generator had required. It now requires at least one sign
  change.
