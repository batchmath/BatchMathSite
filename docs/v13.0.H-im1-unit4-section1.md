## Section 1 practice engines

| Page | Engine | What it practices (from assignments 01A–01F) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1A — Geometric or Not | `geometric-or-not` | Is the sequence geometric (Yes/No)? If so, enter the common ratio. |
| 1B — Common Ratios and Next Terms | `common-ratios-and-next-terms` | The common ratio and the next three terms. |
| 1C — Finding Terms of Geometric Sequences | `finding-terms-of-geometric-sequences` | The first five terms from a₁ and r; or a single term aₙ. |
| 1D — Geometric Growth and Decay Modeling | `geometric-growth-and-decay-modeling` | Practice Type: Growth and Decay Factors; Growth and Decay Word Problems; Mixed. |
| 1E–1F — Section 1 Review | `section-a-review` | Practice Type: Mixed, or any one of 1A–1D. |

**1A — Geometric or Not**
- **Geometric sequences:** ratios are whole numbers, unit fractions, other fractions (3/2, 2/3, 4/3, 3/4) and their
  negatives. Some sequences have fractional terms, such as 100, 50, 25, 25/2.
- **Look-alikes:** arithmetic sequences; a geometric pattern with one term changed slightly (3, 6, 12, 21); squares and
  other quadratic patterns; irregular changes.
- **Arithmetic look-alikes:** the explanation states that the sequence adds the same amount, while a geometric sequence
  multiplies by it.

**1B and 1C: the same variety of ratios, with exact fractional terms**
- An example is 7, 14/3, 28/9, 56/27, whose next three terms are 112/81, 224/243 and 448/729.
- Negative ratios produce alternating signs.
- Term numbering is explicit throughout: fields are labelled a₅, a₆, a₇ and so on.
- The worked solution for aₙ counts the n − 1 multiplications after a₁ and lists every term.

**1D — Geometric Growth and Decay Modeling**
- **Growth and decay factors:**
  - percent increases and decreases (2%–100%);
  - multiplying words such as "doubles", "triples", "is cut in half" and "5 times as large";
  - fractions lost, such as "one third is lost", which gives r = 2/3.
- **Question forms:** students state the common ratio; choose it (the distractors are typical mistakes: the opposite
  change, the percent alone, the bare number); or read a ratio as "growth/decay by p%".
- **Word problems** follow the assignment's models: bacteria, populations, medicine, social-media shares, a machine's
  value, a rumor, a decaying sample, a game prize.
  - "The end of hour 1" is a₁, so "the end of hour n" is n − 1 multiplications later.
  - Answers are whole numbers, and the ratio is asked for exactly when the question says "State the common ratio".

**The Section 1 review** mixes the lessons in the proportions of reviews 01E and 01F: 6 identification, 4 next-terms,
10 term-finding and 4 growth/decay problems out of every 24.

**Behavior.** The engines use the approved IM1 Unit 3 practice system unchanged:
- MathLive answer fields, with the legacy keypad as a fallback;
- a keypad with digits, minus, decimal point and fraction keys;
- automatic focus and the Enter key: Enter checks, and Enter moves on after a correct typed answer;
- the Correct and Attempted counters and Reset Score;
- worked solutions after every answer, with the complete correct response after a wrong one;
- reproducible problems: add `?bm_seed=…` to the address;
- analytics engine IDs `im1_unit4_*`.

**Grading.**
- **Accepted:** every equivalent exact form, for example 3/2, 1.5 and 6/4; a typed minus sign; extra spaces.
- **Rejected:** wrong values, and rounded decimals of repeating values, such as 0.333 for 1/3.

**Files.**
- `assets/im1-unit4-generators.js`: the five engines, computed with exact fractions throughout.
- `tools/build-im1-unit4-pages.mjs`: writes the practice buttons and practice pages for Section 1 only (its `PRACTICE`
  table).
- `assets/im1-unit3-practice.js` was changed in three backward-compatible ways, none of which affect Unit 3 pages:
  - a page may name its generator registry (`BM_UNIT3_PRACTICE.registry`); Unit 3 pages name none and use the Unit 3
    registry exactly as before;
  - Unit 3's Section 2 font styling stays limited to Unit 3;
  - the Yes/No follow-up can request a fraction keypad, for ratios such as −1/2.

## Rebuild and test

```bash
node tools/build-im1-unit4-pages.mjs
```

This writes all 32 Unit 4 pages: the 27 resource pages and the 5 practice pages. Running it again changes nothing.

```bash
npm run qa:im1-unit4
```

This checks 4,000 problems per engine and per Practice Type. Each problem is re-read from its question text and solved
independently with exact fractions. The check also covers grading with equivalent, wrong and rounded answers, the
variety the assignments require, and the review mix.

```bash
BM_PORT=<port> node tools/review-lab/im1/unit4-section1-browser.mjs
```

This answers every engine and Practice Type in the browser, on desktop and on a phone.

```bash
BM_PORT=<port> node tools/review-lab/im1/unit4-browser.mjs
```

This checks the Unit 4 pages, menus, all 56 PDFs and the entry points.

**Expected count changes.** `tools/release-qa.mjs` and `tools/analytics-qa.mjs` now expect 148 engines (143 + 5).

**Service worker.** The 40-entry pre-cache list is unchanged. The Unit 4 engine script is cached on first use, like
other course scripts.
