## A. Graph clipping (Unit 6)

**Defect.** In Area Below and Between Curves, the cubic lobes of y = x³ − 4x + 3 and y = −x³ + 4x + 3 reach
3 ± 16/(3√3) ≈ 3 ± 3.079. The generator's view was fixed at c ± 3 with no padding, so the shading crossed the top and
bottom of the frame.

**Fix (shared, `assets/unit6-core.js` `figure()`).** The frame is now fitted to the region's actual geometry.

1. Each shaded piece's boundaries are sampled across the piece.
2. Every local extreme is refined by golden-section search, so a peak or trough between samples is still found.
3. Intersections and marked points are included.
4. The frame extends, and never shrinks, to hold all of this with a modest padding: 6% of the span, at least 0.25
   units, out to the next half grid step. A generator's view that already fits is kept unchanged.

What is preserved:

- the axis of rotation always inside the frame;
- single-scale solids;
- the compact Area graph caps;
- tick labels (none reads 0).

Two small display fixes came with it:

- x tick labels stay inside the frame when the x-axis is above it;
- a legend entry no longer wraps inside itself.

**Scope.** All Unit 6 graph families were checked, in questions and in explanations:

- area (x and y);
- cross sections;
- solids;
- Area & Volume and Graph Analysis multipart problems;
- both reviews, which use the same engines.

Problem text, answers, checking and distributions are unchanged. 2,700 problems compared seed by seed with v12.9.B
differ only in figures, and 96 of 4,453 figures changed their drawing.

## B. One blank MathLive field for every integral setup

The v12.9.B control showed a pre-built integral: a coefficient box, the integral sign, limit boxes, an integrand box,
dx/dy buttons and add-row controls. It is replaced by **one initially blank, focused MathLive field**. Nothing is
filled in. The student writes the whole setup:

- the integral sign;
- the limits;
- the integrand;
- dx or dy;
- any number in front (including π or 2π);
- sums and differences of integrals.

They can use the keyboard or the BatchMath keypad.

- **Keyboard.** Typing `int` gives an integral with two limit slots (MathLive's shortcut). Tab or → moves between
  slots. Typing `dx` or `dy` ends the integral.
- **Keypad.** The **∫** key inserts an editable definite integral at the cursor. The **dx** and **dy** keys add the
  differential. Only keys that integral setups use are shown: fractions, √, ln, sin, cos, xⁿ, π, e, x, y, parentheses
  and arithmetic, plus a decimal point on calculator setups. Cursor movement, deletion, Clear, undo/redo, Enter and
  Check behave as on every other MathLive page.
- **Where it applies.** Every Unit 6 integral-setup part uses this one shared control (`u6-integral` in
  `assets/unit6-ui.js`):
  - Area Below and Between Curves, non-calculator and calculator, including Choosing dx or dy;
  - Finding Area in Terms of y;
  - Volume by Cross Sections, non-calculator and calculator;
  - Solids of Revolution, non-calculator and calculator;
  - Area & Volume — Multi-Part Practice;
  - both comprehensive reviews.
- **Other Unit 6 answers.** These now use MathLive fields too, through the same adapter: exact values (including
  Piecewise Integrals), decimals, lists, intervals, and expressions in x or y (radius, height, A(x)). Graph Analysis
  problems are unchanged apart from their typed fields becoming MathLive fields.

### Grading (unchanged checker)

The field is an input interface. It does not replace mathematical validation.

1. `assets/mathlive/integral-setup.mjs` finds each integral in the LaTeX: its coefficient, limits, the integrand up to
   the differential, and dx or dy.
2. Each piece is translated by the shared MathLive expression translator.
3. The setup is written as `[coefficient*]int(lower,upper,integrand,x) ± …`. Limits keep their typed digits, so the
   precision of calculator limits is known.
4. `parseSetup` in `assets/unit6-core.js` reads that form into the rows that the **v12.9.B structural checker**
   (`checkIntegral`) grades. The checker itself is unchanged.

So the existing rules still hold:

- Equivalent setups are accepted: equivalent integrands, constants inside or outside, split intervals, reversed
  limits with the opposite sign, and halves of symmetric regions.
- Alternatives are accepted only where the problem lists them: the other variable or method.
- Excluded setups are refused with the method note.
- A setup that only has the right value is refused.
- Common mistakes get their notes: (R − r)², a missing π, unsquared radii.

Incomplete or malformed entries get a message and no grade:

- no integral sign;
- a missing limit, integrand, or dx/dy;
- a constant term;
- a factor after an integral;
- nested integrals;
- unbalanced parentheses;
- more integrals than a "single integral" part allows.

The standard editor fallback (`?editor=legacy`, or if MathLive cannot load) accepts the same linear form typed
directly.

  deferred, so the first question already has MathLive fields.

  progress log.
