## 3. Geometry and mathematics (Node): 835,375 checks, 0 failures

**Shapes, checked with independent formulas** on every drawn cross section (the highlighted one and the outlines):

| Shape | Check |
| --- | --- |
| square | s by s |
| rectangle | s by m·s, with right angles |
| semicircle | every arc point at distance s/2 from the slice's midpoint, so the whole slice is the diameter |
| quarter circle | every arc point at distance s from the lo end, so the slice is a radius; the other radius vertical |
| equilateral triangle | three sides of length s |
| isosceles right triangle, leg | legs s and s, right angle at the lo end, hypotenuse s√2 |
| isosceles right triangle, hypotenuse | hypotenuse s, legs s/√2, right angle at the apex |

**Placement.** Each section:
- lies in the plane of its x (or of its y for slices perpendicular to the y-axis);
- spans its slice from lo(t) to hi(t);
- has the shape's height.

**Areas** (tolerance 10⁻¹⁰ relative)
- The exact profile area equals the engine's k·s², for k = 1, 2, 3, π/8, π/4, √3/4, 1/2 and 1/4.
- The drawn polygon area equals k·s² times the chord factor (0.999598 for the arcs, 1 otherwise).

**Same source as the 2D pictures**, for all 1,440 problems:
- The question's Show Graph figure (non-calculator) and Part A's figure with the slice are drawn from the same region
  object whose boundary functions the descriptor holds, compared by identity.
- The 2D slice is the descriptor's slice. Its segment end points equal the 3D slice's to 10⁻¹².
- Calculator questions draw no figure in the question.

**Area factor.** Typed A(x)/A(y) answers (240 problems) equal k·s² at sample points.

**Volume.**
- The answer's volume (exact value, setup value or calculator value) equals k·∫s² by an independent Simpson integral,
  to 10⁻⁷.
- **Mesh.** Every mesh is:
  - closed: its volume is unchanged when the solid is moved;
  - outward: its volume is positive;
  - equal to the exact volume of its own piecewise-linear sweep, to 2·10⁻⁶ (float32 rounding).
- **Mesh vs integral.** Against chord factor × k·∫s², the largest difference is 9.7·10⁻⁴ (0.097%). It is the same for
  every shape, because it comes only from sampling s(t) at 72 cosine-spaced points per piece. It is largest for y^(1/3)
  (y = x³, sliced on y), whose slope is infinite at y = 0.
- **Convergence.** Doubling the sampling divides the difference by 3.99 or more (worst ratio 0.2504), which is second
  order, as expected. No tolerance was relaxed: the test allows 2·10⁻³ and requires that ratio to be at most 0.3.

**Labels.** The labels are the shape's ("s", "2s", "3s", "d = s", "r = s/2", "r = s"), lie in the highlighted section's
plane, and show no value or integral.

**Descriptor.** It is attached to all three families, never enumerable, and never in the JSON.

## 6. Limitations

- The model is a Beta visual aid. There was no physical-device or assistive-technology testing. Touch was tested with
  real touch events in Chromium's device emulation. Safari and Firefox were not tested.
- **Arcs** are drawn as polygons with their vertices on the circle (64 segments for a semicircle, 32 for a quarter
  circle). The drawn area is 0.0402% below the circle's, which is invisible and accounted for in the tests.
- **Sampling.** The drawn solid samples s(t) at 72 cosine-spaced points per piece. Its volume differs from the exact
  volume by at most 0.097%, at y = x³ sliced on y, where the boundary's slope is infinite.
- **Small sections.** Where a cross section is very small compared with the region (for example s = 0.07), its labels
  overlap it. The student can zoom in.
- **The Comprehensive Review pages** do not show the model. They were left unchanged, as required.
