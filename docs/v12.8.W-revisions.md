Every page has a Practice Type dropdown (Mixed plus each family; the Review also offers each topic and each
difficulty). Answers are typed with engine-specific keypads (functions, intervals with ∪ and ∞, lists, decimals,
money); multiple choice appears only for matching slope fields, equations and solution curves. Multi-part
problems use the staged Part A / Part B / Part C flow with feedback per part. Calculator-active problems carry a
badge and are graded to three decimal places (money to the cent, counts to the nearest whole number).

### Mathematical conventions
- Interval of validity: the largest interval containing the initial x-value on which the solution is defined and
  satisfies the original differential equation (dy/dx = y², y(0) = 1 gives (−∞, 1), not ℝ \ {1}). Endpoints are
  closed only where both the solution and the equation are defined.
- Growth y = y₀e^{kt}, decay y = y₀e^{−kt} with k > 0; Newton's law T − Tₛ = (T₀ − Tₛ)e^{−kt} with k > 0 for both
  cooling and warming (see the curriculum note in the progress log).
- Total distance is the integral of |v| after finding every zero of v; speed increases where v and a have the same
  sign. Rates are integrated only over the times they apply; extreme amounts come from the critical times of the
  net rate and the endpoints.

### Slope Fields
- Segments: drag across each marked point (mouse, pen or finger). Graded by direction with an 11° tolerance; a
  segment and its 180° reversal are the same; vertical segments where the slope is undefined.
- Curves: draw the solution through the initial point over the shown field. Graded against an RK4 reference by
  how close the curve passes to the initial point, its distance from the solution, agreement with the field's
  direction, and how much of the solution it covers.
- After checking, the correct segments or curve appear on the same grid (dashed yellow over the student's solid
  blue, with a legend and red circles on wrong segments). Nothing is shown before checking. Try again redraws
  without changing the score. Keyboard alternatives: type the slopes, or type points along the curve.

## Tests
- `npm run qa:unit5`: generation and answer checks for all six engines, slope-field grading cases, independent
  recomputation for Rate, Motion, Growth and Slope Fields, and the Review distribution audit (also part of
  `qa:nobrowser`).

  draw with a mouse or real touch input; slope-field drawing QA; the equivalence test.
