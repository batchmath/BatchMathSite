# v13.0.D: Transparent / Opaque display for the Unit 6 3D models (Beta)

This experiment adds a display toggle to every interactive 3D model of Unit 6: Volume by Cross Sections (v13.0.B) and
Solids of Revolution (v13.0.C). It is on branch `claude/v13.0d-3d-opacity-toggle`, created from the v13.0.C branch at
`cb9738a`. It is not production: `main` is unchanged and nothing is deployed. No question, answer, grading rule,
explanation or 2D diagram changes.

## What a student sees

**The control.** Every model has a two-button switch, **Transparent | Opaque**, in its bar beside **Reset view**.
- **Transparent** is the default and the look of v13.0.A–C, unchanged.
- **Opaque** gives the solid a fully opaque blue exterior, so its shape is easier to recognize at once.

**What Opaque shows**
- The curved surfaces stay smooth, flat faces stay flat, and the edges and outlines on the surface stay visible.
- Holes stay open: a washer's hole, a shell solid's opening and a cone-shaped opening show through to the grid behind.

**What stays the same in both modes**
- the gold region or base;
- the pink axis of rotation;
- the coordinate axes and grid;
- the labels;
- the yellow highlighted slice.

**The highlighted slice in Opaque mode**
- **Its visible parts** are drawn as before. That includes the yellow rim where the slice meets the surface.
- **Its hidden parts** are drawn faintly: a light yellow film, with a dashed rim. This is the classic hidden-line
  convention, so a student can still see where the slice is and what shape it has, and it is never drawn as if it were
  in front of the surface.
- **Solids of revolution.** The region and the axis of rotation inside the solid get the same faint, dashed treatment.
- **Cutaway.** No cutaway is used, so no geometry is removed or invented.

**Using the switch**
- It is immediate: no regeneration, no rebuilt geometry, no reopened viewer.
- The rotation and zoom stay as they were.
- Reset view still returns to the starting view and keeps the chosen display.
- Mouse, keyboard (Tab, then Enter or Space) and touch all work. The pressed button is marked (`aria-pressed`).
- The choice is kept when the model is hidden and shown again; a new question starts Transparent.

## How it is built

**`assets/unit6-3d/scene-kit.mjs` gets `displayModes()`, shared by both viewers.** Switching changes material states
only.
- **Opaque:**
  - the solid's materials become opaque and write depth;
  - they get a small polygon offset, so the lines lying on the surface win the depth test and don't flicker;
  - "closers" are shown;
  - faint copies of the overlays that the solid now hides are shown.
- **The faint copies** are drawn only where the original is hidden (depth test "greater"):
  - face copies share the original's geometry;
  - line copies are dashed copies of the line's points;
  - they are made once, the first time Opaque is chosen.
- **Transparent** restores every saved material state exactly, and hides the closers and the faint copies.

**`viewer.mjs` (cross sections)**
- The cross-section solid had no floor: its bottom is the gold base, drawn translucently. Opaque mode therefore shows
  a blue floor under the gold base, which is still drawn a little in front, so from below the solid is closed.
- The faint copies are the highlighted section, its rim, the slice and its marks.

**`revolution-viewer.mjs` (solids of revolution)**
- The solid is already closed.
- The faint copies are the region, its outline, the axis rod, and the highlighted disk, washer or shell with its rings,
  radii and slice.

**`model-toggle.mjs`** adds the switch to the model's bar, remembers the choice per model, and applies it when a model
is (re)built.

**`unit6.css`** styles the switch like Reset view.

**`assets/pwa.js` (one line).** The site-wide Enter shortcut, which clicks Next when it is visible, now ignores Enter
pressed on a control inside a 3D model.
- Before this, pressing Enter on Reset view, Show/Hide 3D Model or the new switch after answering went to the next
  question. That was true since v13.0.A; the new keyboard test found it.
- Enter elsewhere behaves exactly as before.

**Test hooks.** For the browser tests, the viewers expose read-only state and a few hooks:
- the display mode and the material states;
- the geometry ids;
- `project` and `hitSolid` (ray casting);
- `look`, to turn the view toward a direction;
- `testGhosts`, to switch the faint copies off and on;
- `snapshot`, which draws the scene and returns the canvas as a PNG (the tests read the canvas itself, not a screenshot
  of the page, so nothing on the page can cover it).

**Version.** The version is 13.0.D in the app, service worker, analytics and Unit 6 page markers.
