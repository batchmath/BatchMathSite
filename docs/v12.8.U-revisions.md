# v12.8.U revisions (from v12.8.T)

The final four Unit 4 integrals engines are rebuilt as typed, exact-answer practice with the math keypad.
The pages keep their URLs and their own titles, SEO and PWA metadata; they now use the shared practice
architecture (the Integrals Using Geometry page is the template).

1. Basic Integrals — 11 families: power rule, radicals and negative powers, expand first, divide each
   term (with and without ln), basic trig and e^x, trig identity rewrites, definite power/root, definite
   trig/exponential, power reduction (sin^2 x, cos^2 x, varied coefficients, combinations and definite
   versions) and a^x (including definite).
2. U-Substitution — 11 families: linear inner functions, polynomial inner functions with a constant
   adjustment, shifted quadratics, trig power chains, composite exponential/log, back-substitution,
   definite linear, definite polynomial/trig, definite back-substitution, and odd powers of sine and
   cosine (save one factor, then sin^2 + cos^2 = 1) including definite versions. No u/du boxes.
   Problems also use t, theta and r, each with its own keypad.
3. Natural Log and Inverse Trig — 8 families: linear denominators, f'/f, tangent/cotangent and
   composites, division (synthetic-division table; non-monic and quadratic divisors), inverse tangent,
   inverse sine, definite logarithms and "log or not". Absolute value bars only where the argument can
   be negative.
4. Mean Value Theorem for Integrals — 70% average value, 30% find c. Two parts: the exact average, then
   every guaranteed c (any order) or Does not apply. Piecewise problems check continuity at the break
   and say when a c only happens to exist. Find-c problems use non-symmetric quadratics, cube roots,
   sqrt x, 1/x^2, 1/x, e^(kx), sin and cos, not only the symmetric quadratic pattern.
5. Answers: + C is required ("Your antiderivative is correct, but you forgot + C."); exact equivalent
   forms are accepted; decimals are rejected; domains and absolute values are respected; inverse trig
   is entered with sin^-1 / cos^-1 / tan^-1 keys. Keypads show only the keys each answer needs.
6. The Unit 4 Comprehensive Review uses the same generators, so its Basic Integrals, U-Substitution,
   Natural Log and MVT topics are typed as well. The other review topics are unchanged.
7. Shared fix (assets/answer-normalization.js): moving a minus sign is now limited to a single whole
   fraction. Before, an answer such as -(1)/(5)cot(5x)+C, or -(25)/(4)pi in Integrals Using Geometry
   submitted with Check Answer, could be rewritten into a different value and marked wrong.
