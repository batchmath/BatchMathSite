# v13.0.E: "Show 3D Model" and a Hide / Show control for the highlighted slice

This experiment changes two things in every interactive 3D model of Unit 6: Volume by Cross Sections (v13.0.B) and
Solids of Revolution (v13.0.C), both with the Transparent / Opaque control of v13.0.D. It is on branch
`claude/v13.0e-3d-cross-section-toggle`, created from the v13.0.D branch. It is not production: `main` is unchanged and
nothing is deployed. No question, answer, grading rule, explanation, 2D diagram, geometry or placement rule changes.

## What a student sees

**The 3D button.** It reads **Show 3D Model**, and **Hide 3D Model** while the model is open. "(Beta)" is gone from the
button and from its accessible name. That name was "Show 3D Model (Beta): R revolved about …" on Solids of
Revolution; it is now "Show 3D Model: R revolved about …", and "Hide 3D Model: …" while the model is open. This applies
on both topics, both pages, and to both models of a two-axis question.

**The new control.** Every open model has a button that hides or shows the highlighted (yellow) slice. It sits in the
model's bar, between **Transparent | Opaque** and **Reset view**. It is named after the slice the model shows:

| Topic | Slice | Button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Volume by Cross Sections | a cross section | **Hide Cross Section** / **Show Cross Section** |
| Solids of Revolution, disks | a disk | **Hide Disk** / **Show Disk** |
| Solids of Revolution, washers | a washer | **Hide Washer** / **Show Washer** |
| Solids of Revolution, shells | a cylindrical shell | **Hide Shell** / **Show Shell** |

- **Why the solids use their own names.** A cylindrical shell is not a cross section, so a "Cross Section" button would
  be inaccurate. For disks and washers the slice's own name also matches the legend: "one disk", "one washer", "one
  cylindrical shell".
- **What is hidden, all together:**
  - for cross sections: the section's face, its rim, the white slice line on the base, its marks (such as the right
    angle mark, or the helper line of a semicircle) and its labels (such as s);
  - for solids of revolution: the disk, washer or shell, its edge circles, the white slice line in the region, its
    radius and height lines, and its labels (R, r, h);
  - in Opaque mode, also the faint dashed copies of the slice's hidden parts.
- **What stays:** the solid, the gold region or base, the pink axis of rotation with its label, the coordinate axes,
  the grid and the axis labels.
- **Its legend entry** ("one cross section: …", "one washer: …") is dimmed while the slice is hidden.

**Behaviour**
- A model opens with the slice shown, as before.
- Hiding and showing is immediate. The geometry is not rebuilt or regenerated, and the rotation, the zoom, the
  Transparent / Opaque choice and the question stay as they were.
- The choice stays:
  - when Transparent and Opaque are switched, in either order;
  - when Reset view is pressed (it resets only the view);
  - when the model is hidden and shown again (as the Transparent / Opaque choice does since v13.0.D).
- A new question starts with the slice shown.
- In a two-axis question each model has its own control.
- Mouse, keyboard (Tab, then Enter or Space) and touch all work. Enter on the control does not go to the next question:
  the v13.0.D exception in `assets/pwa.js` covers every control inside a 3D model.
- On a narrow screen the bar shows:
  1. the help line;
  2. Transparent | Opaque;
  3. the slice button and Reset view.

## How it is built

- **`assets/unit6-3d/viewer.mjs` and `revolution-viewer.mjs`.** Each collects the objects that belong to the
  highlighted slice into one list. `setSlice(visible)` sets their `visible` flag and redraws once. Nothing else is
  touched.
- **`assets/unit6-3d/scene-kit.mjs` (`displayModes`).** A faint hidden-line copy now remembers its overlay and is drawn
  only while the overlay is visible (`sync()`). Hiding the slice therefore hides its Opaque-mode copies too, and
  switching modes keeps that.
- **`assets/unit6-3d/model-toggle.mjs`.**
  - The button text has no "Beta".
  - The accessible name follows the button: "Show 3D Model: …" or "Hide 3D Model: …".
  - It adds the slice button and remembers its state per model, like the display mode. A rebuilt viewer is given that
    state.
  - It dims the slice's legend entry while the slice is hidden.
- **`solids-3d.mjs`.** It passes the slice's name (Disk, Washer or Shell) and the label without "Beta".
  `cross-sections-3d.mjs` uses the default name, Cross Section.
- **`assets/unit6.css`.** The slice button is styled like Reset view; the dimmed legend entry has 45% opacity.
- **Test hooks.** `sliceVisible`, and `sliceParts()`: the visibility of every slice object and of its faint copies.
- **Version.** 13.0.E in the app, service worker, analytics and Unit 6 page markers.
