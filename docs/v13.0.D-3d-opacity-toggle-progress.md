# v13.0.D Transparent / Opaque 3D display: test results

The feature is described in `docs/v13.0.D-3d-opacity-toggle.md`. All results below are from the code on
`claude/v13.0d-3d-opacity-toggle` at `5690309`. After it only review-lab tooling, docs and the route-sweep artifact
change, and the ZIP contains none of them. The branch was created from the completed v13.0.C branch at `cb9738a`.
The seed-by-seed regression, the Node geometry tests and the no-browser chain ran on the working tree just before
`5690309`. That commit added only the test-only `snapshot` hook and a hover colour, which none of them load.
`qa:static`, `qa:pwa` and `qa:analytics` were run again on `5690309` and pass.

## 1. The switch in the browser (Chromium, real pages)

`tools/review-lab/unit6/display3d-browser.mjs` ran at 1366×900 with a mouse and the keyboard, and at 390×844 with touch:
**42 runs, 0 failures, 0 console errors.**

| Engine | 1366×900 | 390×844 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Volume by Cross Sections | all 8 shapes, slices ⊥ x and ⊥ y (16) | all 8 shapes, alternating x and y (8) |
| Solids of Revolution | all 7 families, plus 2 washers with a hole along the whole solid, about y = c and x = c (9) | the same 9 |

Every model was opened, rotated and zoomed (mouse drag and wheel; one-finger drag and a two-finger pinch on the phone)
and then switched. In every run:

- **It opens Transparent.** The control says so (`aria-pressed`), and the solid's material states equal the saved
  v13.0.C states.
- **Opaque is immediate.** One click, tap, or key (Enter or Space) gives:
  - exactly 1 redraw;
  - the same canvas and the same geometry buffers (by id), so nothing was regenerated, rebuilt or reopened;
  - the same camera, so the rotation and the zoom are kept.
- **The solid is opaque.** Its materials are opaque and write depth. A canvas point where a ray cast into the actual
  geometry hits the solid turns from the dark see-through blend into solid blue. For example, (38, 81, 123) becomes
  (76, 168, 255).
- **The highlighted slice stays visible.**
  - Its yellow is on the canvas in Opaque mode.
  - Its hidden parts are drawn: switching the faint hidden-line copies off and on changes 143 to 241,700 pixels.
  - In one phone run, the pinch zoom toward the middle of the canvas left the slice off the screen, in Transparent mode
    too. The solid has isosceles right triangles over the region under y = x³: it is 27 units tall, with a 2-unit
    slice. That run was measured in the starting view, which Reset view gives back in Opaque mode: 168 yellow pixels,
    and 143 pixels drawn for the hidden parts.
- **Holes stay open.** For the two washers whose region stays away from the axis, the view was turned to look down the
  axis. A ray at a fraction of the least inner radius misses the solid's geometry, and its pixel in Opaque mode is not
  blue: it shows the grid or the dark background, or the pink axis label seen end on.
- **Reset view and Transparent.**
  - Reset view in Opaque mode returns to the starting view and keeps Opaque.
  - Transparent restores the saved material states exactly. The picture is then **identical, byte for byte**, to the
    picture before Opaque was first chosen.
- **The choice is kept and released correctly.**
  - Hiding and showing the model again keeps Opaque.
  - Hiding releases every GPU geometry and texture, the faint copies included (0 left).
  - A new question starts Transparent.
- **Layout.** No page is wider than the screen.

**Screenshots, reviewed visually** (desktop, and several on the phone). Examples:
- the cross-section solids are solid blue with their flat and curved faces, and their edges on the surface;
- the yellow section shows its visible edges solid and its hidden ones dashed, over a faint yellow film;
- the washers' and shells' holes show the grid behind them;
- the pink axis and the region inside a solid of revolution are drawn faintly, with dashed outlines.

**Keyboard.** Before this version, Enter on Reset view or Show/Hide 3D Model went to the next question once a part was
answered. This was true since v13.0.A: the site-wide Enter shortcut clicks Next. It now activates the control.
- Checked on an answered calculator question in v13.0.C and in v13.0.D, with Enter on Reset view and on the switch.
- v13.0.C advanced to a new question; v13.0.D stayed on the question.
- Enter anywhere else behaves as before.

## 5. Limitations

- **Testing.** The models are a Beta visual aid. There was no physical-device or assistive-technology testing. Touch
  was tested with real touch events in Chromium's device emulation. Safari and Firefox were not tested.
- **Hidden parts, not a cutaway.** In Opaque mode the highlighted slice inside the solid is shown in the hidden-line
  style (faint film, dashed rim), not by cutting the solid open. A cutaway would remove part of the surface the
  student is looking at, so it was not used. A student who wants to see the slice clearly can switch back to
  Transparent.
- **Faint copies on top of each other.** Where several hidden parts lie behind one another (for example the hidden
  region, axis and washer of a solid of revolution), their faint copies add up and are a little brighter there.
- **Small slices.** In a few tall solids the highlighted slice is small, as in v13.0.B and v13.0.C. Zooming toward the
  middle can leave it off the screen; Reset view brings it back.
- **The Comprehensive Review pages** do not show the 3D models, as before.
