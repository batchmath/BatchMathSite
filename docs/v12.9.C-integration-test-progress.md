## Part A: graph clipping

- **`tools/review-lab/unit6/qa/graph-fit.mjs` (new).** It reads every figure of every Unit 6 family and tier, in
  questions and explanations, from the SVG the page receives. Shaded regions (with their intersections and extremes),
  marked points, slice and radius end points, rotation axes and the graph of f must lie inside the frame with a 2.5%
  margin, and no tick may read 0.
  - **Before the fix:** 44 failures, with the smallest margin at −1.3%.
  - **After the fix:** 7,623 figures from 7,440 problems, **0 failures**, smallest margin 5.3%.
  - The reported cubic (y = x³ − 4x + 3, y = −x³ + 4x + 3) passes with a 6% margin.
- **`qa:unit6`** gained a graph-fit section and the reported case as a fixed regression. It fails on the old code and
  passes now.
- **Unchanged content.**
  - 2,700 problems compared seed by seed with v12.9.B have identical text, prompts and answers. Only figures differ,
    and only 96 of 4,453 changed their drawing.
  - The display scan shows identical answers for 1,120 of 1,120 problems.
  - The deep audit has 0 errors.
- **Browser.** The cubic and the changed figures were rendered in the real practice page at 1366×768 and 390×844.
  They fit the question box with no overflow and no errors.
