## Scope, mathematical safeguards, and architecture

- Newton follows the evidenced integral-to-right-endpoint-sum direction. It does not give delta-x or sample points away in the question, import unsupported inverse/left-endpoint tasks, or evaluate nonpolynomial examples by unsupported finite-sum identities. Polynomial worked solutions expand the actual summand, use sum(1), sum(k), sum(k squared), and take the actual limit; no antiderivative shortcut replaces the requested method.
- Geometry covers the source's rectangles/trapezoids, triangles, quarter/semicircles, vertical/horizontal translations, absolute values, signed regions and composites. Cross-axis problems include unequal regions and cancellation. No new total-area question family or unrelated piecewise graph is imported. SVG scales use equal mathematical units and come from the problem data.
- FTC has 35% ordinary upper/lower-x core, 45% single-bound chain-rule practice, and 20% both-bound synthesis. It includes source-supported products, compositions, rational/trig/exponential/log integrands; students substitute the bound and include its derivative, rather than finding an antiderivative. Source integrands with poles/logarithms are paired with valid bounds and explicit domains. Some literal review intervals cross poles (for example R15 3e); generated analogues retain the taught construction skill on proper, continuous intervals rather than silently introducing improper integrals.
- Rules use consistent signed integral facts, including reversed givens. Adjacent/overlapping intervals, zero-width, multiples, sums/differences, unknown pieces and nested combinations follow the examples. The two synthesis families are staged, so derived interval values support the final combination.
- Keep numbered parent Topic 7 and all classroom resource/video links. Retain its `practice/` URL for FTC with a dedicated FTC identity. Add `integral-rules-practice/` under the same parent, with a separate generator and analytics identity. The old combined generator becomes a compatibility dispatcher for both modes; Comprehensive Review dispatches directly to the two separate new generators.
- Preserve the review's existing eleven-topic mix (each topic 1/11 in mixed mode); replace only the four corrected child factories. This keeps the approved approximation/Sigma and other topic shares stable. No evidence from these attachments justifies reweighting unrelated engines.
