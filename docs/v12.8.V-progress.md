## Root causes

- **Scrollbars.** `overflow-x:auto` makes `overflow-y` compute to `auto`; MathJax boxes for fractions,
  integral signs and limits extend 3–9 px past the line box, so each short equation was a vertical
  scroll box. On main, at 1366×768 with 4 problems per page, 36 explanation lines in the four engines
  and the Review were scroll boxes (plus Unit 1 `.method` and the Newton equations); after the fix, 0
  on all 53 practice pages that share these styles.
- **Inverse trig.** The generator printed the substitution scale k/c unreduced in the argument
  (`\tan^{-1}(4x/2)`), and a few templates printed coefficients and powers of 1.
- **FTC checker.** The keypad serializes 2, x, sec as `2xsec(`. The shared parser
  (`assets/derivative-expression-checker.js`) read the letter run `xsec` as one unknown name and threw,
  so the answer was reported invalid. The FTC checker's trig-notation rewrites start at a word boundary,
  so they also missed `xsin^(2)(…)`. 6,188 of 20,000 generated FTC answers have a factor directly
  before a function. A separate pre-existing defect: a fixed 1e-15 absolute tolerance accepted 2× the
  answer when the answer is near 1e-28 at every probe; the slack is now relative to the median value.
