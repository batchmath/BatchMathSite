CHANGES
-------
- Practice-launch labels now allow balanced two-line wrapping and use a slightly smaller responsive font so long topic-specific names fit the glossy buttons.
- Derivatives Comprehensive Review computational prompts no longer disclose the differentiation method. Method-specific prompts such as “Use logarithmic differentiation” / “Use the chain rule” were replaced by neutral derivative prompts while task-specific distinctions such as second derivative or derivative-at-a-point remain when needed.
- Derivatives Conceptual & AP-Style Review math display was rebuilt to use MathJax-safe question/choice/explanation markup. Shared AP topic-practice choice rendering now supports mixed prose + MathJax and feedback is re-typeset after answers are checked, which applies sitewide to engines using the shared practice shell.
- Limits at Infinity now includes broader logarithmic and exponential composition families at both positive and negative infinity, including ln(algebraic expression), ln(reciprocal algebraic expression), logarithms of radicals/rational expressions, e^(algebraic expression), e^(rational algebraic expression), and exponent-to-zero cases.
- Uploaded Unit 1 Homework Packet and Answer Key are bundled and linked on the Limits & Continuity Comprehensive Review page.
- Shifted cube-root variable-substitution Limits family hardened: a coefficient multiplying x-a is now explicitly grouped in the displayed numerator (for example 2(x-3), never 2x-3 when the solution factors x-3), and the explanation explicitly carries that coefficient through the substitution. The family now also includes fifth-root-value target k=5 examples such as cube-root expressions approaching 5.
