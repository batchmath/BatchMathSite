BatchMath SAT & ACT Math Practice — video worksheet review

1. Extract the ZIP and open the extracted folder in a terminal.
2. Run py -m http.server 8000 on Windows or python3 -m http.server 8000 on macOS
   or Linux.
3. Open http://localhost:8000/sat-act-math/ in your browser. Keep the terminal open.

The ZIP uses a bundled CSV served from localhost. The production Google Sheets
URL will be configured separately after the owner supplies it.

- Open the numbered questions. Check an answer on #001 or #003 to see both
  explanation buttons. Their videos are cued at 0:19 and 0:20. Press Play in the
  embedded player when ready. Internet access is needed for YouTube and MathJax.
- Open Show Written Solution before answering to try an unscored reveal, or open
  it after checking an answer. The three Pending questions offer written
  solutions after checking an answer.
- Edit assets/sat-act/video-preview.csv to try a status change. Reload after the
  five-minute cache refresh window or open the review in a fresh browser profile.

The import-ready sheet template and setup instructions are in docs/sat-act/.
This archive omits the classroom PDFs listed in OMITTED-PDFS.txt. Use the full
checkout for website QA and an authorized production deployment.
