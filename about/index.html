About BatchMath

Free mathematics instruction and practice built from the classroom outward.

I have been teaching high school mathematics for 18 years, including AP Calculus for more than a decade. BatchMath grew from the lessons, explanations, practice, and review materials I use with students. The goal is straightforward: make high-quality math resources easy to find, easy to use, and genuinely useful for learning.

Built for Students

BatchMath combines targeted interactive practice with videos, notes, assignments, answer keys, and review material so students can move between learning a skill and actually using it.

Built from Teaching

The site focuses on the places where students repeatedly need support: foundational Integrated Math 1 skills, algebra and trigonometry preparation for calculus, and a complete AP® Calculus AB course sequence.

Free and Direct

Resources are designed to open quickly without accounts, subscriptions, or unnecessary distractions. Practice should feel like practice—not like navigating a platform.

Always Developing

BatchMath continues to grow as new classroom materials, practice engines, AP® free-response resources, and instructional tools are added and refined.
