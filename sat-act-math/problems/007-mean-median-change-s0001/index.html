#007 Problem Solving · Statistics

  • Statistics and Probability › Mean, median, range, standard deviation; outliers

Original BatchMath practice question · not an official SAT® or ACT® question

The test scores of 7 students are listed below.

\[62,\ 72,\ 77,\ 82,\ 94,\ 97,\ 99\]

The score \(99\) was recorded incorrectly. The correct score is \(86\).

After the score is corrected, how do the mean and the median change?

Written Solution

Show the step-by-step solutionHide the step-by-step solution

Your answer: A

The sum falls by \(99 - 86 = 13\), so the mean falls by \(\dfrac{13}{7}\) points, while the median stays \(82\).

Your answer: B

The median is the 4th of the 7 ordered scores, \(82\), before and after the change, because \(86\) is still greater than \(82\).

Your answer: C

The sum of the scores falls by \(13\), so the mean falls from \(\dfrac{583}{7}\) to \(\dfrac{570}{7}\); only the median stays the same.

  1. Step 1 One change, two measures

    Careful: changing a score does not always change the median. The median uses position; the mean uses every value.

  2. Step 2 The median uses position

    With \(7\) ordered scores the median \(M\) is the 4th: \(82\). The corrected score \(86\) is still above it, so the 4th score does not change.

    \[M = 82\]

  3. Step 3 The mean uses every value

    Shortcut: no need to find either mean. The sum drops by \(13\), so the mean drops by \(\dfrac{13}{7}\).

    \[99 - 86 = 13\]
    \[\dfrac{13}{7} \approx 1.86\]

  4. Step 4 Conclusion

    The mean decreases; the median stays the same.

Answer

D · The mean decreases, and the median stays the same.

Key Idea

The mean uses every value. The median depends only on the middle position, and here the corrected score is still above the middle.

Why the other choices are wrong
  • A · The mean stays the same, and the median decreases.: The sum falls by \(99 - 86 = 13\), so the mean falls by \(\dfrac{13}{7}\) points, while the median stays \(82\).
  • B · Both the mean and the median decrease.: The median is the 4th of the 7 ordered scores, \(82\), before and after the change, because \(86\) is still greater than \(82\).
  • C · Neither the mean nor the median changes.: The sum of the scores falls by \(13\), so the mean falls from \(\dfrac{583}{7}\) to \(\dfrac{570}{7}\); only the median stays the same.
