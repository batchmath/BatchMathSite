#005 Find the Radius of x² + y² − 4x + 4y = 73

  • Coordinate Geometry › Equations of Circles
  • Medium

Original BatchMath practice question · not an official SAT® or ACT® question

The equation of a circle in the \(xy\)-plane is shown.

\[x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 4y = 73\]

What is the radius of the circle?

Your answer: A

\(2 \sqrt{2}\) is the distance from the origin to the center \((2, -2)\), not the distance from the center to a point on the circle.

Your answer: B

This uses only the constant \(73\); completing the square adds \(4\) and \(4\) to the right side too, so \(r^2 = 73 + 4 + 4 = 81\).

Your answer: D

\(81\) is \(r^2\), the right side of \((x - 2)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 81\); the radius is its square root, \(9\).

  1. Step 1 Goal: standard form

    Standard form: \((x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2\). Careful: \(73\) is not \(r^2\) yet.

  2. Step 2 Complete the square

    Take half of each coefficient and square it.

    \[(-4 \div 2)^2 = (-2)^2 = 4\]
    \[(4 \div 2)^2 = 2^2 = 4\]

  3. Step 3 Add to both sides

    Add \(4\) and \(4\) to both sides. Each group is now a perfect square.

    \[(x^2 - 4x + 4) + (y^2 + 4y + 4) = 73 + 4 + 4\]
    \[(x - 2)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 81\]

  4. Step 4 Read the center and radius

    The center is \((2, -2)\) and \(r^2 = 81\).

    \[r = \sqrt{81} = 9\]

  5. Step 5 Shortcut: radius only

    Shortcut: for the radius alone, the right side is all you need.

    \[r^2 = 73 + 4 + 4 = 81\]

  6. Step 6 The traps

    The traps: \(\sqrt{73}\) skips the added \(4 + 4\), and \(81\) is \(r^2\), not \(r\).

Answer

C · \(9\)

Key Idea

Complete the square in \(x\) and \(y\), adding the same amounts to both sides; the right side is \(r^2\).

Why the other choices are wrong
  • A · \(2 \sqrt{2}\): \(2 \sqrt{2}\) is the distance from the origin to the center \((2, -2)\), not the distance from the center to a point on the circle.
  • B · \(\sqrt{73}\): This uses only the constant \(73\); completing the square adds \(4\) and \(4\) to the right side too, so \(r^2 = 73 + 4 + 4 = 81\).
  • D · \(81\): \(81\) is \(r^2\), the right side of \((x - 2)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 81\); the radius is its square root, \(9\).
