#004 Original Price After a 20% Discount and 6% Tax

  • Ratios, Proportions, and Percentages › Percentages and Percent Change
  • Medium

Original BatchMath practice question · not an official SAT® or ACT® question

A store discounts a jacket by \(20\%\). Then a \(6\%\) sales tax is added to the discounted price. The final price is \(\$76.32\).

What was the original price of the jacket?

Your answer: A

This applies the opposite percents to the final price: \(76.32 \times 1.2 \times 0.94 \approx 86.09\), rounded to the nearest cent. Undoing a \(20\%\) discount means dividing by \(0.8\), not multiplying by \(1.2\).

Your answer: B

This treats the changes as one \(14\%\) decrease: \(76.32 \div 0.86 \approx 88.74\), rounded to the nearest cent. Successive percents multiply instead: \(0.8 \times 1.06 = 0.848\).

Your answer: D

This undoes only the discount, treating the final price as if it were the price before tax: \(95.40 \times 0.8 = 76.32\). The \(6\%\) tax was also added, so it must be undone too.

  1. Step 1 Careful: the percents do not combine

    Careful: this is not \(14\%\) off. The \(20\%\) comes off the original price; the \(6\%\) tax is added to the smaller, discounted price.

  2. Step 2 Each change is a multiplier

    With \(P\) the original price: \(20\%\) off leaves \(80\%\), and \(6\%\) tax makes \(106\%\).

    \[0.8(1.06)P = 76.32\]

  3. Step 3 Calculator time

    Calculator: setting up the equation is the skill. Let the calculator do the decimals.

    \[P = 76.32 \div (0.8 \times 1.06) = 90\]

  4. Step 4 Check by working forward

    Run \(\$90\) through both changes.

    \[90 \times 0.8 = 72\]
    \[72 \times 1.06 = 76.32\]

  5. Step 5 The trap: adding the percents

    The trap: \(14\%\) off would multiply by \(0.86\), not by \(0.848\).

    \[76.32 \div 0.86 \approx 88.74\]

Answer

C · \(\$90.00\)

Key Idea

Percent changes multiply. To undo them, divide by the multipliers.

Why the other choices are wrong
  • A · \(\$86.09\): This applies the opposite percents to the final price: \(76.32 \times 1.2 \times 0.94 \approx 86.09\), rounded to the nearest cent. Undoing a \(20\%\) discount means dividing by \(0.8\), not multiplying by \(1.2\).
  • B · \(\$88.74\): This treats the changes as one \(14\%\) decrease: \(76.32 \div 0.86 \approx 88.74\), rounded to the nearest cent. Successive percents multiply instead: \(0.8 \times 1.06 = 0.848\).
  • D · \(\$95.40\): This undoes only the discount, treating the final price as if it were the price before tax: \(95.40 \times 0.8 = 76.32\). The \(6\%\) tax was also added, so it must be undone too.
