#003 Find k So the System Has No Solution

  • Systems of Equations › Number of Solutions of a Linear System
  • Medium

Original BatchMath practice question · not an official SAT® or ACT® question

In the system of equations below, \(k\) is a constant.

\[6x - 3y = 1\]
\[18x + ky = 10\]

For what value of \(k\) does the system have no solution?

Your answer: B

With \(k = -3\), the ratios \(\dfrac{6}{18}\) and \(\dfrac{-3}{-3}\) are not equal, so the lines have different slopes and intersect at exactly one point.

Your answer: C

With \(k = -1\), the ratios \(\dfrac{6}{18}\) and \(\dfrac{-3}{-1}\) are not equal, so the lines have different slopes and intersect at exactly one point.

Your answer: D

With \(k = 9\), the ratios \(\dfrac{6}{18}\) and \(\dfrac{-3}{9}\) are not equal, so the lines have different slopes and intersect at exactly one point.

  1. Step 1 No solution means parallel lines

    No solution means the two lines never meet: they are parallel. Watch the signs when you match the equations.

  2. Step 2 Shortcut: scale the coefficients

    Shortcut: parallel lines have \(x\)- and \(y\)-coefficients in the same ratio.

    \[6 \times 3 = 18\]
    \[k = -3 \times 3 = -9\]

  3. Step 3 Check the constants

    If the constants scaled by \(3\) too, both equations would be the same line.

    \[1 \times 3 = 3 \neq 10\]

  4. Step 4 See it: equal slopes

    Solve each equation for \(y\): both slopes are \(2\), but the \(y\)-intercepts differ.

    \[y = 2x - \dfrac{1}{3}\]
    \[y = 2x - \dfrac{10}{9}\]

  5. Step 5 The trap: the wrong sign

    The trap: \(k = 9\) has the wrong sign. The slopes are then \(2\) and \(-2\), so the lines cross.

    \[y = -2x + \dfrac{10}{9}\]

Answer

A · \(-9\)

Key Idea

No solution means parallel lines: the \(x\)- and \(y\)-coefficients share one ratio, but the constants do not.

Why the other choices are wrong
  • B · \(-3\): With \(k = -3\), the ratios \(\dfrac{6}{18}\) and \(\dfrac{-3}{-3}\) are not equal, so the lines have different slopes and intersect at exactly one point.
  • C · \(-1\): With \(k = -1\), the ratios \(\dfrac{6}{18}\) and \(\dfrac{-3}{-1}\) are not equal, so the lines have different slopes and intersect at exactly one point.
  • D · \(9\): With \(k = 9\), the ratios \(\dfrac{6}{18}\) and \(\dfrac{-3}{9}\) are not equal, so the lines have different slopes and intersect at exactly one point.
