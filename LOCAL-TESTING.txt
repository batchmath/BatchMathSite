BatchMath v13.0.B — complete interactive 3D Volume by Cross Sections (experimental test build, not production)

Extract bm-v13.0.B.zip.
Open a terminal inside the single folder bm-v13.0.B.
Run exactly:

py -m http.server 8000

(On macOS or Linux: python3 -m http.server 8000)
Open http://localhost:8000/ in your browser.
Keep the terminal open while testing. Stop the server with Ctrl+C.

Where the 3D model is: AP Calculus AB > Unit 6 > Volume by Cross Sections.
- Non-Calculator Practice: every question has "Show 3D Model (Beta)" right under the "Show Graph" button.
- Calculator Practice: the region is not shown until Part A is checked (students graph it on their calculators).
  Check Part A (right or wrong); its explanation shows the 2D picture, and "Show 3D Model (Beta)" appears right under it.
Every cross-section shape and both directions are supported. Drag to rotate; scroll or pinch to zoom; Reset view.
(Answer a question and press Next Question, or reload, for another one.)
