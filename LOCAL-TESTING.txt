BatchMath v13.0.A — experimental interactive 3D cross sections (test build, not production)

Extract bm-v13.0.A.zip.
Open a terminal inside the single folder bm-v13.0.A.
Run exactly:

py -m http.server 8000

(On macOS or Linux: python3 -m http.server 8000)
Open http://localhost:8000/ in your browser.
Keep the terminal open while testing. Stop the server with Ctrl+C.

To see the 3D model: AP Calculus AB > Unit 6 > Volume by Cross Sections > Non-Calculator Practice.
Choose the Practice Type "Perpendicular to the x-axis" and press Next until the cross sections are squares,
then press "Show 3D Model (Beta)" under the question. Drag to rotate; scroll or pinch to zoom.

The website, practice engines, PDFs, local MathLive runtime and fonts, and the local three.js library are included.
Normal MathJax rendering and YouTube video playback require internet.
This is an experimental test prerelease, not a production deployment.
