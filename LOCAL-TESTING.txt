BatchMath v13.0.A — experimental interactive 3D cross sections (test build, not production)

Extract bm-v13.0.A.zip.
Open a terminal inside the single folder bm-v13.0.A.
Run exactly:

py -m http.server 8000

(On macOS or Linux: python3 -m http.server 8000)
Open http://localhost:8000/ in your browser.
Keep the terminal open while testing. Stop the server with Ctrl+C.

To see the 3D model: AP Calculus AB > Unit 6 > Volume by Cross Sections > Non-Calculator Practice.
It appears for square cross sections perpendicular to the x-axis. Choose the Practice Type "Perpendicular to the
x-axis"; when a question has square cross sections, press "Show 3D Model (Beta)" under it. Drag to rotate; scroll or
pinch to zoom. (Answer a question and press Next Question, or reload, for another one.)

These links open directly on a supported question (the same question every time):
http://localhost:8000/ap-calculus/unit-6-applications-integration/topics/volume-by-cross-sections/non-calculator-practice/?bm_seed=3d68   (y = 4 - x^2 and y = 0)
http://localhost:8000/ap-calculus/unit-6-applications-integration/topics/volume-by-cross-sections/non-calculator-practice/?bm_seed=3d16   (a triangle)
http://localhost:8000/ap-calculus/unit-6-applications-integration/topics/volume-by-cross-sections/non-calculator-practice/?bm_seed=3d72   (y = e^x, y = 1, x = 1)
http://localhost:8000/ap-calculus/unit-6-applications-integration/topics/volume-by-cross-sections/non-calculator-practice/?bm_seed=3d128  (y = sqrt(x), y = x - 6, y = 0: two pieces)
http://localhost:8000/ap-calculus/unit-6-applications-integration/topics/volume-by-cross-sections/non-calculator-practice/?bm_seed=3d139  (y = 2 sqrt(x), x = 1, y = 0)

The website, practice engines, PDFs, local MathLive runtime and fonts, and the local three.js library are included.
Normal MathJax rendering and YouTube video playback require internet.
This is an experimental test prerelease, not a production deployment.
