BatchMath v13.0.G — complete IM1 Unit 3 video integration; Version 13 3D controls retained

Open a terminal inside the BatchMathSite working copy.
Run exactly:

py -m http.server 8000

(On macOS or Linux: python3 -m http.server 8000)
Open http://localhost:8000/ in your browser.
Keep the terminal open while testing. Stop the server with Ctrl+C.

NEW IN v13.0.E: the button is now called "Show 3D Model" (no "Beta"); it becomes "Hide 3D Model" while the model is open.
Every open 3D model has a new button beside Transparent | Opaque and Reset view that hides or shows the highlighted
(yellow) slice with everything that belongs to it: its outline, the slice line, its marks or radii, and its labels
(s, R, r, h). It is called "Hide Cross Section" on Volume by Cross Sections, and "Hide Disk", "Hide Washer" or
"Hide Shell" on Solids of Revolution (named after the slice the model shows); it then reads "Show ...".
- The slice is shown when a model first opens. Hiding and showing it is immediate: the view (rotation and zoom), the
  Transparent / Opaque choice and the question stay. It works in both Transparent and Opaque, and the choice stays when
  you switch between them, press Reset view, or hide the model and show it again. A new question starts with it shown.
- Mouse, keyboard (Tab to it, Enter or Space) and touch.

FROM v13.0.D: every 3D model (both topics below) has a "Transparent | Opaque" switch beside Reset view.
- Transparent (the default) is the look of v13.0.A-C. Opaque shows the solid's outside fully opaque, so its shape is
  easier to see; parts of the highlighted slice (and, for solids of revolution, the region and the axis) that the solid
  hides are then drawn faintly, with dashed outlines. The switch is immediate: the view (rotation and zoom) stays.
- It works with the mouse, the keyboard (Tab to it, Enter or Space) and touch. Reset view keeps the chosen display.
  The choice is kept when the model is hidden and shown again; a new question starts Transparent.

VOLUME BY CROSS SECTIONS (v13.0.B): AP Calculus AB > Unit 6 > Volume by Cross Sections.
- Non-Calculator Practice: every question has "Show 3D Model" right under the "Show Graph" button.
- Calculator Practice: the region is not shown until Part A is checked (students graph it on their calculators).
  Check Part A (right or wrong); its explanation shows the 2D picture, and "Show 3D Model" appears right under it.
Every cross-section shape and both directions are supported. Drag to rotate; scroll or pinch to zoom; Reset view.
(Answer a question and press Next Question, or reload, for another one.)

SOLIDS OF REVOLUTION (v13.0.C): AP Calculus AB > Unit 6 > Solids of Revolution.
- Each model shows the region (gold), the axis of rotation (a pink line labelled with its equation), the solid (blue) and
  one highlighted disk, washer (R and r) or cylindrical shell (r and h). Drag to rotate; scroll or pinch to zoom; Reset view.
- A model appears where the 2D picture of the region and that axis is shown: under the graph when the question shows it;
  otherwise (every calculator question, and non-calculator questions without a graph) in the explanation of the part that
  first draws that rotation, after the part is checked. Questions about two axes have one model per axis, with its part.
